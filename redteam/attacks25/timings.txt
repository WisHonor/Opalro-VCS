RT26 timings, 2026-11-18, in the no-network sandbox (4 CPUs, 4 GB), one import at a time.
Release builds in the sandbox: milestone 11 (fd0eb95) in /tmp/t11, v0.2 (main, 3f71add) in
/tmp/t02. measure.sh gives the wall time, the bytes read (rchar) and the peak memory (VmHWM).

packbuy.py <dir> <packs> <buy> <w> <k> <ln> <n> <commits>: every object loose; two top trees,
A and B, taken in turns; each holds `buy` one-file trees (the same in both) and w folders that
reach, k levels down under names of ln letters, a folder of n files that all differ between A
and B. packs > 0 adds that many packs of one blob each (fillpacks.py).

  repository                                   milestone 11                 v0.2
  pb0: 0 packs, 0 buy, 100 5 760 99000, 4       stopped, 6.65 s (comparing)  0.22 s
  pb1: 4095 packs, 3000 buy, same, 40 commits   imported, 135.74 s           0.87 s
  pb2: pb1 without the 4095 packs               stopped, 5.78 s (comparing)  0.43 s

So 4,095 packs of one blob each (about 5 MB of files) turned a stop at 5.8 s into an import
that compares 3.4 s for every commit: by the counts, pb1's allowance is about 405 GB and each
comparison spends about 634 MB, so it stops only after about 640 commits, about 36 minutes.
  pb3: as pb1 with 100 buy                       stopped, 83.96 s, "15 GiB"    -
